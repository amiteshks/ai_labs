Company Workplace Policies
1. Payroll Policy

Employees are compensated on a bi-weekly basis through direct deposit.
Payments are issued according to the company payroll schedule and will be deposited directly into the employee’s designated bank account.

Employees are responsible for ensuring their banking information is accurate and up to date to avoid payment delays.

2. Leave Request Policy

Employees must submit a leave request for approval before taking time off.

All leave requests should be submitted through the company’s designated system or communicated to the appropriate manager in advance. Approval from a supervisor or manager is required prior to the leave being taken unless there is an emergency situation.

3. Internet Usage Policy

The company provides broadband internet access to support business operations.

Employees are expected to use the company internet for work-related tasks only. Personal usage that interferes with productivity, security, or company operations is not permitted.

The company reserves the right to monitor internet usage to ensure compliance with company policies and security standards.

4. Break Policy

Employees are allowed to take one hour break during the workday.

Breaks should be scheduled in coordination with the employee’s manager to ensure work responsibilities and team coverage are maintained.

5. Workplace Conduct and Respect

All employees are expected to interact with each other with respect and professionalism at all times.

The company promotes a workplace culture built on:

Mutual respect

Professional communication

Inclusiveness

Collaboration

Harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior toward colleagues will not be tolerated.

6. Policy Compliance

All employees are expected to read, understand, and follow the policies outlined in this document. Failure to comply with company policies may result in disciplinary action.
